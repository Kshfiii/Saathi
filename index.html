Why Saathi

Safety designed for how panic actually feels.

In an emergency, every millisecond matters. Fumbling into a bag, unlocking a smartphone with face recognition, or navigating through apps is practically impossible under extreme stress. Saathi replaces complex digital steps with one instinctual physical action.

Two-Tap Discretion

A deliberate double-press prevents accidental bumps in your bag or pocket while giving you instant covert activation without looking down or alerting bystanders.

Silent Confirmation

A subtle, gentle haptic pulse lets you know your distress signal and live GPS coordinates have been sent to your guardian network without making a sound.

Your Circle in Sync

Instantly alerts your pre-configured family, friends, or campus security with your real-time pinned route, battery level, and ambient situational updates.

Our firmware requires two distinct, firm presses within an 800ms window. A single bump or continuous compression in a tight pocket or bag will not trigger an alert.

Saathi connects seamlessly via ultra-low-power Bluetooth to your smartphone. Your phone can stay zipped inside your backpack or pocket—even locked with screen off—and will automatically relay the SOS broadcast and GPS location.

Under normal everyday carry, the high-density LiPo cell lasts up to 6 months per charge. It recharges via standard USB-C in under 45 minutes, and our companion app notifies you when battery dips below 20%.
